# New repository checklist

1. Replace `YOUR-USERNAME` and `YOUR-REPOSITORY` in `_quarto.yml` and `_brand.yml`.
2. Add `assets/documents/NavarreteC_CV.pdf`.
3. Copy your real blog posts into `posts/` and teaching materials into `classes/`.
4. Replace the sample Goodreads CSV with your current export.
5. Preview with `quarto preview`.
6. Render with `quarto render`.
7. Initialize the new repository and push `main`.
8. Enable GitHub Pages from `main` and `/docs`.
